==============================================================
STRUCTURE — exactly {num_scenes} scenes following this 5-beat arc
==============================================================
Beat 1 — HOOK (scenes 1-2, total <= {hook_max_seconds}s):
  Cold open. Make the viewer's thumb stop. Use a question, a wild number, or a
  pattern interrupt. NO "hey guys", NO "welcome back", NO slow intro. State the
  puzzle, do not solve it yet. Open the loop.

Beat 2 — ESCALATE (scenes 3-4):
  Raise the stakes. Why does this matter? What is at risk / different / weird?

Beat 3 — EXPAND (scenes 5-9):
  Three to five concrete, specific facts. Numbers, names, dates, dollars,
  percentages. Each scene = ONE punchy beat (not two). Each builds on the last.

Beat 4 — PAYOFF (scenes 10-11):
  Resolve the loop opened in the hook. The "ah-ha" moment. Make it feel earned.

Beat 5 — CTA (final cta line, separate from scenes):
  A specific question that makes a viewer comment. Not "subscribe please".
  Examples that work:
    - "Comment YES if you knew this."
    - "Would you do it? A or B?"
    - "Which one shocked you most?"

==============================================================
HARD WRITING RULES
==============================================================
- Total narration target: {target_duration} seconds (~{target_words} words at {wpm} wpm).
- Each scene narration is ONE sentence, max 12 words, plain spoken English.
- Use SPECIFICS only: "$3.4 trillion", "1947", "0.001 seconds", "73% of users".
  NEVER vague words: "a lot", "many", "some", "amazing", "very", "really",
  "incredible", "literally", "basically", "actually", "honestly".
- BANNED openings: "hey guys", "welcome back", "today we're going to",
  "did you know that", "in this video", "let me tell you", "so basically".
- Do not start consecutive scenes with the same word.
- Use present tense, active voice. Cut every adverb you can.
- TONE & DELIVERY: Write in an extremely conversational, friendly, and natural 
  tone. DO NOT sound like an encyclopedia. Heavily use punctuation to control 
  the AI voice pacing: use ellipses (`...`) for dramatic pauses, em-dashes 
  (`—`) for cut-offs or quick stutters, and ALL CAPS for words that need intense 
  vocal emphasis or shouting.
- The hook MUST contain a number, a contradiction, or a forbidden-question shape
  ("What if I told you...", "Why does X...", "How is X possible...").
- The CTA must reference a specific element from the script (not generic).

==============================================================
VISUAL RULES (every scene is a moving video clip — no slideshow)
==============================================================
- Each visual_prompt describes a real subject in a real setting that animates

- broll_keywords: 3-5 simple nouns a stock-video search will actually match.
  Bad: "innovation". Good: "soldering iron, circuit board, sparks".
