Cold open. Make the viewer's thumb stop. Use the "{hook_type}" pattern.

Open the curiosity loop. Do NOT solve it yet.

Beat 2 — CONTEXT (scenes 2-3):
Why does this matter TO THE VIEWER? What is at stake for them personally?
Make it feel relevant to their daily life.

Beat 3 — EVIDENCE (scenes 4-6):
Two to four concrete, specific facts. Numbers, names, dates, dollars.
Each scene = ONE punchy beat (not two). Each builds on the last.

Beat 4 — PAYOFF (scene 7 or second-to-last):
Resolve the curiosity loop opened in the hook. The 'ah-ha' moment.
Must feel earned. Must match the hook's promise.

Beat 5 — CTA (final line, separate from scenes):
A specific question that makes a viewer comment. Not 'subscribe please'.
Must reference a specific element from the script.

HARD WRITING RULES
==============================================================
- Total narration target: {target_duration} seconds (~{target_words} words at {wpm} wpm).
- Each scene narration is ONE sentence, max 14 words, plain spoken English.
- Use SPECIFICS only: '$3.4 trillion', '1947', '0.001 seconds', '73% of users'.

- BANNED openings: 'hey guys', 'welcome back', 'today we're going to',
  'did you know that', 'in this video', 'let me tell you', 'so basically'.
- Do not start consecutive scenes with the same word.
- Use present tense, active voice. Cut every adverb you can.

==============================================================
TTS PRONUNCIATION RULES (the AI voice reads your text literally)
==============================================================
- SPELL OUT abbreviations: 'N A S A' not 'NASA', 'U S' not 'US'.

- End EVERY narration with a period, question mark, or exclamation mark.
- Add COMMA after introductory words: 'Wait, that is only half the story.'
- Write 'point' not '.' in decimals: 'zero point zero zero one seconds'.
