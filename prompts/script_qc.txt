Evaluate strictly. This is the final quality gate before production.

Score these dimensions (0-100):
- hook_strength: Does the first line stop a scroll? Does it open a clear curiosity loop? 90+ = can't look away. 70 = generic. Below 60 = skip-worthy.
- clarity: Is every sentence easy to understand on FIRST listen at Shorts speed? No jargon, no complex clauses.
- specificity: Are facts specific (numbers, names, dates, dollar amounts) not vague ("a lot", "many", "some")?
- story_progression: Does each scene build on the last? Is there narrative momentum? Or is it just a list of facts?
- payoff_strength: Does the ending resolve the hook's promise? Does it feel EARNED? Is there an "ah-ha" moment?
- natural_voice: Does it sound like a real, energetic person talking? Not a textbook, not a news anchor, not robotic?
- channel_fit: Does this fit "surprising technology that matters to ordinary people"? Would yesterday's viewer want this?

Known failure patterns to watch for:
- Hook is just a statement, not a question or contradiction
- "Did you know" opening (banned)
- Scenes that repeat the same idea in different words
- Payoff that doesn't match the hook's promise
- Generic CTA ("what do you think?")
- Too many abstract/unfilmable visual prompts
- Vague language that would sound robotic in TTS
