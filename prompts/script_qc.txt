Evaluate strictly. This is the final quality gate before production.

Score these dimensions (0-100):
- hook_strength: Does the first line stop a scroll and open a curiosity gap? 90+ = can't look away. 70 = generic. Below 60 = skip-worthy.
- content_density: Is every single sentence packed with meaning? Zero fluff. No wasted words.
- factual_grounding: Are the claims backed by specific numbers, dates, and entities? No vague assertions like "many people" or "a long time ago".
- novel_information: Does this tell the viewer something they likely didn't already know?
- non_redundancy: Does the script avoid repeating the same concept in different words across scenes?
- spoken_naturalness: Does it sound like a real, energetic person talking? Not a textbook, not a news anchor, not robotic? No AIisms ("delve", "explore").
- payoff_strength: Does the ending resolve the hook's promise? Does it feel EARNED? Is there an "ah-ha" moment?
- channel_fit: Does this fit "surprising technology that matters to ordinary people"? Would yesterday's viewer want this?

Known failure patterns to watch for:
- Hook is just a statement, not a question or contradiction
- "Did you know" opening (banned)
- Scenes that repeat the same idea in different words
- Payoff that doesn't match the hook's promise
- Generic CTA ("what do you think?")
- Too many abstract/unfilmable visual prompts
- Vague language that would sound robotic in TTS
